Turn showing up into a magazine.

You bring the work. We keep the time and the place. The deadline closes itself.

What a club adds up to:

  • A few real friends.
  • Work you actually finished.
  • Issues with your name in them.

What it takes, every time:

  • A challenge, so everyone’s pulling one way.
  • An hour, so it’s a habit and not a plan.
  • A portfolio, so you can see you got better.
